# 2026-10-10 joinery

## Goal
Cut the frame into printable corner sections with joinery at the seams

## Steps
<!-- `blend history witch-frame` has the full step log; summarise the arc here. -->
- Decided: butterfly keys, mid-side seams.
- Measured the back-face strip at the seams (rabbet edge to bowed outline): 7.8-10.8 mm over the key length, which sets the key width.
- 4: `builders/40_sections.py` rewritten. Pieces are live `GN_SectionPiece` cuts of Frame (quadrant box minus `Cut_Keys`). Keys are 32 mm bow-ties, width fitted per seam (N 6.0, S 6.0, E 5.5, W 5.8), 5 mm deep recess, 0.15 clearance, keys 0.2 below flush. A ray-cast assert keeps >= 1 mm wall beside each recess.

## Outcome
- Print collection: Piece_TR/TL/BL/BR (139.7 x 177.8 x 40, fits the 256 bed), Key_N/S/E/W, Clip_01-08. `check --collection Print --strict` is clean (watertight). Exported 16 parts as stl/obj/glb.
- Keys are small (waist ~3.3 mm) because of the narrow back strip. Glue on the seam faces carries the load; the keys align and clamp.

- Review: the node trees got labelled stage frames for reading in the GN editor.
- Units: the file was correct (mm, 1 BU = 1 mm), but the viewport clip was 1 m. `fit_viewport` now runs in build_all.
- Print pack: `just print-pack witch-frame` gives print/parts.blend + 16 STLs + a manifest, 363.7 cm³, all watertight and fitting the bed. Handed off to bambi.
