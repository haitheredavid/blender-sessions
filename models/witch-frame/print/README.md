## Assembly
- Dry-fit the 4 corner pieces on a flat surface, face down.
- Glue a loose tenon into the top pieces first: Tenon_N into TL/TR's top seam, Tenon_E into Piece_TR, Tenon_W into Piece_TL; Tenon_S into the bottom seam. Each tenon's pointed (gable) edge goes up, away from the back face. Epoxy or slow CA fills the 0.15 mm gaps (wood glue does not bond PETG). Print the Coupon pack first to confirm the clearance.
- Glue the seam faces and close each half along x (top pair over Tenon_N, bottom pair over Tenon_S), then close the halves along y over Tenon_E/W. Clamp flat, face down.
- Drop in glazing (2 mm), art on its 3 mm backboard, then screw the 8 turn clips over the backboard.
